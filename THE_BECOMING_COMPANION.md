# The Becoming — Human-Factors Companion

*The Becoming* is the human-factors companion to the AI Accountability Standards architecture.

The technical stack in this repository defines the architecture: AI Black Box logging, drift detection, the Persistent Identity Layer, ZeroTX, Missing CVR, Distributed Oversight, Early Warning Channels, deployment tiers, and check-ride protocols.

The book explains why that architecture matters.

It connects the standards to aviation safety, crew resource management, black boxes, cockpit voice recorders, inspection discipline, automation bias, mode confusion, and the human responsibility problem that sits underneath every serious AI deployment.

## Relationship to the Architecture

The book is not the product being sold.

The architecture is the saleable asset: a set of blueprints for AI accountability infrastructure that an engineering partner, acquirer, or standards organization can implement.

The book serves as the narrative and human-factors layer around that architecture. It explains the lived pattern-recognition that led to the system and the responsibility framework behind it.

## How to Read the Stack

- **AI Black Box / AIBB** — records what the machine did.
- **Missing CVR** — captures the human/AI decision context that ordinary logs miss.
- **Loop Detector** — identifies human over-reliance, fixation, abdication, and automation traps.
- **Persistent Identity Layer / PIL** — preserves decisions, scar tissue, and continuity across AI sessions.
- **ZeroTX** — keeps sensitive personal or organizational data local by design.
- **Ethics Floor** — defines minimum accountability constraints below which the system should not operate.
- **Check-Ride Protocol** — tests AI systems the way aviation tests pilots: not once, but repeatedly, under changing conditions.

## Why Aviation

Aviation learned the hard way that complex machines require more than operator confidence.

They require instruments, audit trails, independent records, failure-mode taxonomies, crew coordination, recurrent testing, and a human captain who remains answerable for the outcome.

AI systems now need the same kind of accountability layer.

That is the bridge *The Becoming* makes.
