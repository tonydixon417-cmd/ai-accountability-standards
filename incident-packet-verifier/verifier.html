Incident Packet Verifier

INC-1 v0.1 · runs entirely offline · no account, no upload, no network

Select the packet folder (manifest.json, events.jsonl, and its companions).
Nothing leaves this machine. The verification runs in this page.

What this does not prove

  • That the record is complete. It proves the events present were not altered after sealing. A record that was never written cannot be checked.
  • That the model was honest. A faithful record of a confident wrong answer is still a faithful record.
  • That the human was competent. It reports whether an approval exists, not whether anyone read the thing.
  • That redaction was minimal. It reports that redactions were committed to, and how many.
  • That the timestamp is real until the detached time-stamp token is validated by an independent tool against the imprint shown below.
Verification is mechanical. Interpretation is the reader's. This page will not give you a score.
