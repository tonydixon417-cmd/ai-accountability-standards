# Removal notice — version 2.5.3, 28 October 2026

## What was removed

Two files present in versions 2.5.1 and 2.5.2 of this archive are not present in 2.5.3:

1. `Contrail_Acquisition_Brief.md` — a sale and transaction document prepared for buyer review. It contained the commercial position, the proposed exit structure, and product pricing.
2. `Ethics_Floor_Healthcare_Pitch_v1.0.md` — a one-page sales brief addressed to prospective customers.
3. `PIL_Token_Compressed_v1.0.md` — a private operator profile containing personal and third-party detail. See below.

## Why

This archive is a public technical record: standards, frameworks, a reference implementation, and evaluation material. A document prepared for a buyer is not part of that record, and publishing one under a license that permits commercial reuse and redistribution is inconsistent with marking it confidential. The same applies to customer sales material.

Removing them from the current version is the correction. **Earlier versions remain in the DOI history and cannot be retracted**; anyone who downloaded 2.5.1 or 2.5.2 may hold copies. This notice exists so the record states plainly what changed and when.

## What did not change

No technical content was altered. No framework, standard, protocol, reference implementation file, or evaluation artifact was edited. Version 2.5.3 is 2.5.2 minus the two documents above.

## Standing recommendation, not yet applied

The record is licensed CC-BY-4.0. Creative Commons advises against using its licenses for software, because they grant no patent rights and do not distinguish source from object code. The conventional arrangement is a permissive software license (Apache-2.0 or MIT) for the reference implementation and CC-BY-4.0 for the prose documents. Applying that split is a rights decision for the author and has deliberately not been made here.

## Additional corrections in this version

- `PIL_Token_Compressed_v1.0.md` was removed entirely. It was a private operator profile, not a standard, and it carried personal health, family, and financial detail plus the names and employers of third parties who never consented to publication. It should never have been in a public archive. The Institute contact address replaces it as the point of contact for this work.
- `Distributed_Oversight_Model_v1.0.md` carried the line an authorship line that credited drafting tooling as co-author. It now credits the author alone. Public artifacts in this archive are authored by their author; tooling used in drafting is not a public co-author.
